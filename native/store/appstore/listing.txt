Description (4000 characters max):
Flush Finder helps you find a restroom before you stall.

Open a satellite map, zoom in, and tap pins at gas stations, grocery stores, restaurants, parks, and public stalls. Each pin shows an overall 1-to-5 toilet rating, optional notes like Customers only or Ask for key, and written reviews from verified accounts.

Guests can browse the map and read reviews. Creating an account and confirming email is required to rate, write a critique, or add a new restroom. New user pins stay personal until enough other accounts add the same public restroom nearby.

Earn points for verified reviews and spend them on bathroom-pun titles in your account.

Location is used on your iPhone to show nearby stalls and walking directions. It is not stored as a travel history. We may sell or share personal information and use it for personalized ads. Turn that off anytime in Account → Privacy.
