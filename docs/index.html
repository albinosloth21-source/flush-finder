Privacy policy

Flush Finder privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This is a static copy of the in-app policy. The copy Play should link is the live site’s /privacy page. Age 16+ stays in sync with MIN_AGE in src/lib/constants.ts.

Who we are

Misfit Software operates Flush Finder. On Google Play the developer account name is MisfitSoftware.

1. Age

Flush Finder is for people who are 16 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. The age checkbox at sign-up is a self-declaration, not an identity check.

2. Location is optional

You can browse without sharing location. If you turn it on, it centers the map and finds nearby restrooms. We do not keep a GPS history. Map tiles and search go to Esri, Photon, OpenStreetMap, and the public OSRM router so the map can answer. Feedback you send may store a map center rounded to about a kilometer.

3. Accounts

Accounts are optional. Sign-in, when configured, is Google, X, Apple, email and password, or an email one-time code. We store name, email, provider account id, a password hash if you use a password, and session data that can include IP address and browser type. One-time codes last until they are used or they expire.

4. Reviews and points

Reviews, titles, and points are stored with your account. After deletion, published reviews stay as “Deleted user.” Restrooms only you added are removed.

5. Selling data

“Sell my data & personalized ads” is off by default. Only someone who confirms they are 16 or older can turn it on. This version has no advertising SDK and does not send your account to a data broker. Turning the switch on records permission. The policy will be updated before any sale starts.

6. Retention

Account data stays until you delete the account. Sessions end when they expire, you sign out, or you delete the account. One-time codes expire. The host and database process data to run the app.

7. Delete your account

Use Account → Titles → Delete my account, or the public page /delete-account on the live site.
